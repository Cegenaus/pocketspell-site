How a night goes

  1. Pick a keeper

    Each keeper starts with their own weapon and style of play.

  2. Hold back the dark

    The night lasts ten minutes. Enemies pour in from every side, faster as the clock runs.

  3. Grow stronger

    Collect light to level up. Each level adds a weapon, a power, or upgrades something you already carry.

  4. Reach dawn

    Spend the embers you earned on permanent upgrades, then go back out and last longer.
