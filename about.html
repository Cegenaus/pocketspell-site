About Pocketspell

A small independent studio making games for phones.

Pocketspell Studios started with a simple idea: the best phone games feel like a little spell you carry around. You open them on a break, in a waiting room, or on the couch, and they pull you right back in.

We're a small team of players first. We spend our evenings in roguelites, RPGs, sports sims and fantasy football leagues, and those are the kinds of games we want to make. Our first game, Lantern Keeper, grew out of hundreds of hours with survivor-style games. Next up is Waiver Wire, a fantasy football season you can play anytime.

Friends play every build before you do, and their feedback shapes what ships. If you have an idea or find a bug, tell us.

What you can expect from us

  • No account neededDownload and play. We don't ask who you are.
  • Your progress stays with youSaves live on your phone, and our games play offline.
  • Real answersEmail us and a person who works on the game writes back.
